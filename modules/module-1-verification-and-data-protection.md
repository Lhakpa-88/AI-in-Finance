# Module 1 — The Trustworthy Assistant: Verification & Data Protection
**Duration: 2.0 hours** · Part of *AI for Finance Professionals* (20-hr PBL course)

> Standalone facilitator brief. See `../course-design/delivery-methodology.md` for the session rhythm used in every module.

## Problem scenario
A finance officer pasted the full grant budget — staff salaries and a beneficiary list included — into a free consumer AI to "tidy the formatting." What are the exposures, and what should the organisation do, now and structurally?

## Learning objectives
Participants can classify data by sensitivity, recognise "shadow AI," and apply a pre-prompt gate before using any tool.

## Concepts
- Data classification: public / internal / confidential / personal
- "Shadow AI" and why it is the sector's top finance risk
- Human-in-the-loop review
- Prompt hygiene: redact, anonymise, use synthetic data
- Free consumer tools vs. enterprise/data-protected accounts

## Hands-on
Take a realistic (synthetic) donor budget and practise **de-identifying** it before use. Build a personal red-line list of "never paste" data types.

## Verification focus
The two-question gate before every prompt:
1. Would I email this to a stranger?
2. Will I check the output against source?

## Deliverable
A one-page **Data Red-Line Card** for the desk (template in `../resources/data-red-line-card.md`).

## Facilitator notes
This is the course's safety backbone. Everything after it assumes these reflexes are in place.
