# Module 3 — Donor Financial Reports & Narratives
**Duration: 2.0 hours** · Part of *AI for Finance Professionals* (20-hr PBL course)

> Standalone facilitator brief. See `../course-design/delivery-methodology.md` for the session rhythm used in every module.

## Problem scenario
A quarterly financial report is due. The figures are final, but the variance narrative and a plain-language summary for a non-finance programme lead are not.

## Learning objectives
Participants can turn numbers into audience-appropriate narrative and explain variances, while keeping every claim traceable to the figures.

## Concepts
- Numbers into narrative
- Explaining variances
- Tailoring the same financials to donor / board / programme audiences
- Summarising long donor guidelines

## Tools
General-purpose assistants (widely regarded as the best all-round tools for drafting memos, reports, and summaries).

## Hands-on
Feed AI a synthetic budget-vs-actual table and produce three versions of the same variance explanation for three audiences.

## Verification focus
Every number in the narrative must trace back to the table. AI-written explanations of *why* a variance happened are hypotheses to confirm, never facts.

## Deliverable
A three-audience variance narrative set.
