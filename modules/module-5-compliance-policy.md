# Module 5 — Compliance & Policy Interrogation
**Duration: 2.5 hours** · Part of *AI for Finance Professionals* (20-hr PBL course)

> Standalone facilitator brief. See `../course-design/delivery-methodology.md` for the session rhythm used in every module.

## Problem scenario
A programme wants to charge a cost that may be unallowable under the donor's rules **and** may carry a local tax/VAT/TDS implication. You need a defensible answer by end of day.

## Learning objectives
Participants learn the critical skill of **grounding** — giving the AI the authoritative source text and making it reason within that text, rather than trusting its memory.

## Concepts
- Retrieval over recall: AI does **not** reliably know your specific donor rules or your country's tax law
- Feeding source documents and asking questions against *that text only*
- Catching invented rules

## Tools
A citation-first research assistant for public regulatory questions; a document-grounded assistant where you paste/upload the actual donor guideline or tax provision.

## Hands-on
Take a real (public) donor cost-principle document and a local tax provision. Use AI to answer an eligibility question — then trace every claim back to a clause. Deliberately catch the AI inventing a rule.

## Verification focus
No compliance answer leaves the room without a clause reference. If the AI can't cite it, treat it as unverified.

## Deliverable
A worked eligibility determination with source clauses attached.

## Facilitator notes
Highest-stakes module for finance credibility — budget the full 2.5 hrs. **Localise** to the cohort's real donors and tax jurisdiction. This is where experienced staff realise AI is a reasoning aid over *their own sources*, not an oracle.
