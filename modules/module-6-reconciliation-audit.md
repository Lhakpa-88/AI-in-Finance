# Module 6 — Reconciliation, Audit Prep & Anomaly Spotting
**Duration: 2.0 hours** · Part of *AI for Finance Professionals* (20-hr PBL course)

> Standalone facilitator brief. See `../course-design/delivery-methodology.md` for the session rhythm used in every module.

## Problem scenario
The external audit is in three weeks. You want to find the timesheet gaps, coding errors, and odd transactions *before* the auditor does.

## Learning objectives
Participants can use AI to build audit-readiness checklists, surface anomaly leads, and draft management-response language — keeping detection human-led on protected data.

## Concepts
- Drafting an audit-readiness checklist
- Describing patterns to look for
- Summarising findings and drafting response language

## Hands-on
Feed AI a synthetic transaction extract and ask it to flag anomalies and explain its reasoning. Then critique which flags are real vs. noise.

## Verification focus
AI-flagged "anomalies" are leads, not conclusions. Every flag is investigated, not actioned blindly.

## Deliverable
An audit-prep checklist + a template management-response paragraph.
