# Module 2 — Building & Stress-Testing Budgets
**Duration: 2.0 hours** · Part of *AI for Finance Professionals* (20-hr PBL course)

> Standalone facilitator brief. See `../course-design/delivery-methodology.md` for the session rhythm used in every module.

## Problem scenario
A proposal budget is due in 48 hours. You have the donor template, a scope of work, and a staff grade scale — and a blank sheet.

## Learning objectives
Participants can use AI to draft a budget structure and justification narrative, and to *pressure-test* a budget for gaps and unallowable items — without letting AI own the arithmetic.

## Concepts
- Drafting line structures and budget narrative with AI
- Sense-checking assumptions and ratios
- Spotting missing cost categories and unallowable costs

## Tools
General-purpose assistant for structure and narrative; spreadsheet-embedded AI for formulas and totals — with the hard caution that **AI is never the source of the final arithmetic.**

## Hands-on
Groups build a modular grant budget from a scope of work, then ask AI to "act as a sceptical donor reviewer" and find weaknesses.

## Verification focus
Re-compute every total independently. Confirm cost categories against the donor's actual budget notes, not the AI's assumptions.

## Deliverable
A draft grant budget + the AI-generated "reviewer critique" they addressed.
