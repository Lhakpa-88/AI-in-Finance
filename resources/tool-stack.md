# Suggested Tool Stack

> The AI-tools landscape moves quarterly. Treat every named product as **illustrative** and re-check before each cohort. Verified as of August 2026.

A sensible, non-profit-budget stack:

## General-purpose assistants (the workhorses)
E.g. **ChatGPT**, **Claude**. Best all-round tools for drafting memos, reports, and policies; quick calculations; and summarising documents. Free tiers are enough to learn on; a paid/enterprise tier is worth it wherever **data protection** matters.

## Citation-first research
E.g. **Perplexity**. Searches live sources and shows citations — useful for public regulatory/tax questions where traceability matters (Module 5).

## Spreadsheet-embedded AI
E.g. **Microsoft Copilot** in Excel/Word/Outlook, or **Google Sheets** AI. Best where the organisation already runs that ecosystem; strong for formulas, pivots, and first-draft reports without switching tools. Requires a licence.

## Ledger-embedded AI
E.g. **QuickBooks Intuit Assist**, **Xero's assistant**. Only relevant if the organisation already uses that accounting platform; handles reconciliation suggestions and anomaly flags inside the ledger.

## Deliberately out of scope for most small/medium nonprofits
Enterprise autonomous-finance platforms (order-to-cash, high-volume AP automation, SOX tooling). Name them so participants recognise them, but don't build the course on tools they won't have.

## The rule that outlives every tool
**The professional verifies and signs; the AI drafts.**
