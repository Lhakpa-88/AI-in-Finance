# Data Red-Line Card

_Print it. Keep it by your screen. Check it before every prompt._

## Never paste into an AI tool
- Staff salaries or payroll detail
- Beneficiary names, locations, or any personal data
- Donor-confidential financial information
- Government IDs, passport/citizenship numbers
- Bank account or card numbers
- Anything you couldn't email to a stranger

## Before every prompt — two questions
1. **Would I email this to a stranger?** If no, de-identify it first.
2. **Will I check the output against source?** If no, don't use the output.

## The rule
**AI drafts. I verify. I sign.**
